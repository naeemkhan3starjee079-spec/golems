# LinkedIn 2026 Algorithm Guidelines

> Extracted from Aviv Levi's viral LinkedIn post (Feb 2026). These are data-backed rules for LinkedIn content strategy.

---

## Core Principle

LinkedIn exposure dropped ~50% in 2025. The algorithm now prioritizes **engagement depth** over vanity metrics. Working by "inspiration" gets you buried. Working by data builds reach.

---

## The 11 Rules

### 1. Dwell Time > Likes
**The algorithm counts seconds, not clicks.** It measures how long the user's thumb stops on your post. A post scanned in 3 seconds = dead post.

- **Do:** Write content that makes people stop scrolling and read
- **Don't:** Write short shallow posts optimized for quick likes
- **Metric:** Aim for 30-60 second read time minimum

### 2. PDF/Carousels = Highest Engagement (6.6%)
Documents force horizontal scrolling, which boosts dwell time massively.

- **Do:** Create carousel/PDF posts with step-by-step breakdowns
- **Don't:** Rely on plain text posts (they get scanned and skipped)
- **Format:** 5-10 slide carousels with one key idea per slide

### 3. Zero-Click Strategy
LinkedIn penalizes posts with external links. They don't want users leaving the platform.

- **Do:** Put ALL value inside the post itself
- **Do:** Put links in the FIRST COMMENT or in your Bio
- **Don't:** Put links in the post body (immediate exposure penalty)

### 4. Mobile-First Writing
72% of LinkedIn users scroll on mobile in ~90-second sessions.

- **Do:** Short lines, lots of whitespace
- **Do:** Max 12 words per sentence
- **Don't:** Write "wall of text" paragraphs
- **Don't:** Assume desktop reading experience

### 5. Personal Profiles > Company Pages
Company pages get 1-2% organic reach. People connect with people, not logos.

- **Do:** Post from your personal profile
- **Do:** Use Employee Advocacy (team members sharing)
- **Don't:** Waste energy on company page posts

### 6. The Golden Hour (First 90 Minutes)
The algorithm tests your post on a small sample in the first 90 minutes. No engagement = buried.

- **Do:** Block calendar time to engage right after posting
- **Do:** Reply to every comment in the first 90 minutes
- **Don't:** Post and walk away

### 7. Saves > Comments > Likes (Hierarchy)
New engagement hierarchy: Like = 1 point, Comment = 10 points, Save = 100 points.

- **Do:** Write practical content people want to save ("guides", "checklists")
- **Do:** Write content people forward to their boss in DMs
- **Don't:** Optimize for likes only

### 8. 5 Posts Per Week Minimum
Consistency is math. Under 2 posts/week = invisible. Over 5 = flywheel that 3x exposure.

- **Do:** Commit to 5 posts/week for 3-6 months
- **Don't:** Post sporadically based on "inspiration"
- **Timeline:** 3-6 months to spin up the flywheel, then ROI is massive

### 9. Authentic Photos Only
Generic stock photos make people skip. Authentic (even unpolished) photos get 6.5x more engagement.

- **Do:** Use real photos of yourself, your workspace, your team
- **Don't:** Use stock photos or AI-generated images
- **Exception:** Carousels/PDFs (these are the document format, not photos)

### 10. Fixed Post Structure
Every post follows: Hook (3 lines) -> Meat (value) -> CTA (conversation starter).

```
HOOK (3 lines - must stop the scroll)
[line break]

MEAT (the actual value - practical, specific, save-worthy)
[line break]

CTA (conversation starter at the end)
```

- **Hook:** Controversial take, surprising number, personal story opener
- **Meat:** Numbered tips, step-by-step, lessons learned, practical framework
- **CTA:** Open question that invites comments

### 11. CTA in Comments, Not Post Body
Put the "want more? DM me" or link in the first comment, not the post body. This forces readers to:
1. Read the full post (dwell time)
2. Scroll to comments (engagement signal)
3. Actively engage to get the resource

This signals genuine interest to the algorithm, not bait.

## Post Template

```
[HOOK - 3 lines max, stop the scroll]
[Surprising stat / personal confession / contrarian take]

[BLANK LINE]

[MEAT - the value]
[Numbered list or step-by-step breakdown]
[Each point: short sentence + specific detail]
[Max 12 words per sentence]
[Lots of whitespace between points]

[BLANK LINE]

[CTA - conversation starter]
[Open question that invites discussion]
[NOT a link, NOT "follow me"]
```
